Install on Windows in three steps

  1. Run the installer

    It installs for your Windows account only, so it doesn't ask for administrator rights.

  2. Pick your options

    A desktop shortcut, a Start menu entry, and whether Vibe starts minimized when you sign in. You can change them later.

  3. Add the browser extension

    To send browser downloads and videos to Vibe, load the extension in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox. The extension guide shows how.

Good to know

  • Updates install themselves. Vibe checks once a day and updates silently, keeping your settings. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Windows SmartScreen may warn that the publisher is unknown until the installer is code-signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
  • Video streams are joined into one .mp4 when ffmpeg is on your PATH. Without it you get the original pieces.
  • Unattended installs: msiexec /i VibeDownloadManager-{{version}}-x86_64.msi /qn. Add DESKTOPSHORTCUT="" or AUTOSTART="" to skip those options.
  • macOS: drag the app onto Applications. It isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
  • Linux: unpack the tarball and run ./install.sh. It installs for your user only and adds Vibe to your applications menu.
  • macOS and Linux packages are newer than the Windows installer and don't update themselves yet.
